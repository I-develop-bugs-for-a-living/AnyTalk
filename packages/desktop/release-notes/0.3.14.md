### New features
- Sharing a single window on Windows 11 sends only that app's sound, not everything else playing on your computer.
- Pick which camera to use during a call, and flip between the front and back camera on phones.
- Sign in with a passkey, or use a passkey as a second factor.
- People outside a call see a preview of a stream when they hover over the streamer.

### Changes
- The two-factor settings show whether it's turned on and ask before removing the authenticator.

### Bug fixes
- Sharing a single window on Windows no longer sends other apps' sound.
- Your own camera preview shows on iPhone.
- Leaving a call on a phone no longer shows an error and freezes the buttons.
