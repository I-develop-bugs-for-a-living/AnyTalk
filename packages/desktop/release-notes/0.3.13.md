### Changes
- Call encryption is stricter: keys only go to people who are allowed in the call. Someone who is kicked, banned or loses access to the call can't listen in anymore, and people who join can't decode what was said before they joined.
- Each account now has its own call encryption key, so several accounts used in the same app can't be linked to each other.
- Links that only pretend to be GIFs can no longer reveal your IP address, and GIF search only talks to your own server.

### Bug fixes
- The streamer's name shows on stream tiles again, and it appears and hides together with the viewer count and zoom controls.
- Full screen stream controls no longer disappear when a game takes focus.
- Streams of Java games like Minecraft show the game's name.
- The controls of other streams no longer show through an enlarged stream, and the pop-out button no longer hides behind the full screen controls.
